Why We Started J & B Plumbing

J & B Plumbing was founded by two lifelong Colorado residents who grew up in Parker and understood firsthand how important honest, dependable service is in a small community. After years of working in the plumbing industry, they saw a growing need for a company that treated customers like neighbors — not numbers.

The owners built J & B Plumbing on a simple idea: do the job right, communicate clearly, and always leave a home better than they found it. They believed that plumbing shouldn’t be stressful or confusing, and that families deserve a team they can trust every time something goes wrong.

Today, J & B Plumbing continues to operate with those same values. Every repair, every installation, and every inspection is done with care, professionalism, and pride. The company’s mission is to serve Parker with reliable workmanship and genuine respect — because this community is home.
